JoyousChat is a mental health habit-building app. Specifically, the habit I am targeting with this app is journaling. The goal is to utilise an LLM model to synthesize the user's personality type, target areas they would like to work on (relationship, career, daily life, etc), and psychological models to generate effective journaling entries on a daily basis. It is important to note here that AI is not used to converse or advise users but rather analyse their intake test responses to provide a personalised experience. 
Additionally, a lot of nowadays mental-health apps use flashy designs, smooth UI, but no actual research to back up their methods. This app is going to change that. I aim to deliver both smooth and effective experience to the users that uses psychological research in human motivation, behavior change, and habit building to provide long-term results and better access to mental health help tools.
